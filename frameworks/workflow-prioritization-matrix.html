Tune to your org
Two independent reads on every candidate workflow. Priority decides the order it ships. Exposure decides how much the machine is allowed to do. It never changes the priority, only the posture.
Step 1 · Score each workflow (1-5 per axis)
01 · IMPACT
Impact
Revenue, margin, or velocity moved if this runs well
02 · VOLUME
Volume
How often it runs: frequency × people it touches
03 · TIME
Time drain
Manual hours it consumes across the team today
04 · FEAS
Feasibility
Data availability + how cleanly the logic maps to rules
Priority = Impact × Volume × Time × Feasibility   ·   Exposure (money / comp / customer stakes) sets the automation posture separately

Posture · Automate: system acts, human spot-checks  ·  Assist: system drafts, human decides  ·  Human-owned: system informs only
Step 3 · Sequence into 90 days
Days 0-30
Foundation & quick wins
Wire the governance layer to the systems of record. Ship 1-2 low-exposure, top-priority automations (competitive flagging, renewal risk) to prove the pattern.
Days 31-60
Highest-leverage
Attack the biggest time-drain × volume workflows (bottom-of-funnel review, coaching prep). Stand up the human-gate review loop.
Days 61-90
Scale & govern
Templatize so new workflows ship as configuration, not rebuilds. Formalize the audit trail. Keep money / comp / customer workflows human-owned.
The governance rule Score sets the order. Exposure sets the posture. Anything touching money, comp, or a customer stays human-owned regardless of how high it scores. That single policy is what keeps the whole system auditable, and it's why reps and managers trust it enough to adopt it.
