MEDDPICC Deal-Scoring Framework

How committed deals get scored, flagged, and made defensible line by line: the rubric behind the Intelligent Forecast workflow. Every signal is deterministic and built only from the deal's own CRM fields.

Two scoring layers run against every committed deal: qualification depth (MEDDPICC) and deal hygiene (behavioral signals in the record). Each fires a binary flag with a fixed owner. The model explains and drafts the next step; a human confirms anything that moves the number.

Roll-up · from signals to a risk tier
● Red · at risk
A committed deal carrying multiple high-weight flags. Surfaced to the forecast call with the reasons attached.
● Amber · watch
Isolated or medium-weight flags. Rep owns the fix; tracked but not haircut.
● Green · clean
No flags fired against the region-normalized thresholds. Counts at full confidence.
  • Region-aware normalization. Durations and thresholds compare each deal to its region's norm, so EMEA's longer proposals aren't over-flagged and NA's fast-region stalls aren't missed.
  • Deterministic and binary. Every flag either fires or it doesn't, from the deal's own values: no probabilistic black box on a number the CFO has to defend.
The rule that keeps it auditable Every flag maps 1:1 to a signal on this page, and every threshold lives in config. The model only explains why a flag fired and drafts the next step; it never sets or clears a flag. A manager can verify any flag against the CRM in seconds, which is exactly what makes the forecast defensible to the board. Money, comp, and customer decisions stay human-approved by policy.
